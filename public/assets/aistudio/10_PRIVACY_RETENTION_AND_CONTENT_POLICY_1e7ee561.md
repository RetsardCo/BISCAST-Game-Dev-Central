# Privacy, Retention, and Content Policy

## 1. Data minimization
Collect only information needed to authenticate contributors, present portfolios, moderate submissions, and administer events.

Do not expose institutional email addresses publicly by default.

## 2. Public versus private fields
Public fields may include display name, approved biography, batch, portfolio links, published projects, credits, and awards. Private fields include authentication email, moderation notes, internal audit data, and any verification evidence.

## 3. Consent
Before publishing:
- Obtain consent for public profile information.
- Confirm contributors have permission to share game media and documentation.
- Obtain permission for team credits and photographs where required.
- Make clear that external links are hosted by third parties.

## 4. Retention and deletion
Create a documented process for:
- Profile visibility changes.
- Account deletion requests.
- Removing personal data while preserving legitimate historical credits where permitted.
- Correcting attribution.
- Removing content that infringes rights or exposes confidential information.
- Retaining moderation/audit records for a defined period.

**TBD:** retention periods and legal/institutional requirements must be approved by the responsible institution. Do not invent a retention duration.

## 5. Content rules
Do not publish:
- Confidential or restricted academic material.
- Content the submitter does not have rights to share.
- Personal information without consent.
- Malicious links or executable downloads hosted by the platform.
- Harassing, discriminatory, or otherwise prohibited content.

## 6. Reporting and takedown
Provide a contact/report path. Record the report, review it, and document the outcome. For urgent privacy or rights issues, temporarily unpublish while reviewing.
